Post your original videos to TikTok, straight from your computer.

DIANA is a web tool for creators. Log in with TikTok, choose a video you own, set the title, visibility and interaction options, and publish it to your own TikTok account.

Open the tool

How it works

  1. Log in with TikTok and approve access. DIANA shows the nickname of the account that will receive the post.
  2. Choose a video from your computer and preview it.
  3. Write the title, choose who can watch, and choose whether to allow comments, duets and stitches. Disclose commercial content if it applies.
  4. Confirm TikTok's declaration and press Post. DIANA shows the publish status until TikTok finishes processing. It can take a few minutes for the video to appear on your profile.

What DIANA does and does not do

DIANA only posts to the account you log in with, only the video you choose, and only after you confirm. It never adds watermarks, logos or text to your content. We do not sell or share your data. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
