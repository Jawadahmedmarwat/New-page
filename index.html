Send your videos to your TikTok drafts, straight from the web.

DIANA is a small web tool for creators. Pick a video you own on your computer, and DIANA delivers it to your own TikTok account as a draft, ready for you to edit and post in the TikTok app.

Open the tool

How it works

  1. Log in with your TikTok account and approve access.
  2. DIANA shows your TikTok name and avatar so you know which account is connected.
  3. Choose a video from your computer and press Upload.
  4. Open TikTok. The video is waiting in your inbox as a draft.

What DIANA can and cannot do

DIANA only uploads to the account that you log in with. It never posts without you: every video lands as a draft and you decide whether to publish it. We do not sell or share your data. Details are in our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
