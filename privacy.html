Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

DIANA is a web tool that lets creators send their own videos to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what data DIANA handles, why, and how you can remove it.

Data we handle

When you log in with TikTok, DIANA receives, with your approval:

  • Your TikTok display name, avatar and open ID (scope user.info.basic).
  • An access token and a refresh token that let DIANA act on your account within the permissions you approved (video.upload, and video.publish where posting is enabled).
  • Your posting options from TikTok, such as your nickname, allowed privacy levels and maximum video length.

When you choose to post, the video file and the title, privacy and interaction settings you select are sent to TikTok. DIANA does not keep a copy of your video after it has been delivered to TikTok.

Your browser stores a session identifier and the name and avatar of the connected account, so the page can remember you. DIANA does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the features you request: showing which account is connected, and sending the video you selected to that account. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.

Who processes it

TikTok receives the content you choose to post, under TikTok's own terms and privacy policy. DIANA's pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and its login and upload service runs on Cloudflare, which stores your tokens on our behalf.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and profile details are kept only while your account is connected. You can delete them at any time by pressing Remove next to your account in DIANA, and you can also revoke DIANA's access in your TikTok app settings. On request, we will confirm deletion of any remaining data within 30 days.

Security

Tokens are stored server-side, never in the page, and traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. No online service is perfectly secure, so we limit what we store to what the features need.

Children

DIANA is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to use TikTok in their country, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
